# Quality standards

Apply this framework proportionately to an asset's risk and intended use.

## Core checks

- **Purpose and scope:** the problem, users, intended outcome and exclusions are clear.
- **Instructions:** steps, inputs, outputs and assumptions are usable and internally consistent.
- **Testing:** representative cases and failure cases have explicit success criteria and recorded results.
- **Verification:** factual claims, calculations, citations and consequential outputs have an appropriate checking method.
- **Human oversight:** consequential decisions identify a responsible person and confirmation point.
- **Privacy and confidentiality:** data minimisation, authorised use and handling constraints are visible.
- **Evidence integrity:** the asset forbids fabricated sources, quotations, results or evidence and exposes uncertainty.
- **Accessibility and inclusion:** language, format and interaction do not create avoidable barriers or exclusions.
- **Limitations:** known weaknesses, unsuitable uses and model/tool assumptions are documented.
- **Maintenance:** a maintainer, review date, version and meaningful change history are present.

## Educational checks

Where relevant, examine:

- pedagogical validity and the evidence or rationale for the method;
- constructive alignment among outcomes, learning activity and assessment;
- whether the cognitive demand is appropriate and remains visible;
- assessment integrity, authorship and opportunities to demonstrate learning;
- responsible AI use, including disclosure and appropriate learner agency;
- risks of substituting fluent output for learning, judgement or evidence.

## Minimum maturity evidence

| Status | Minimum expectation |
| --- | --- |
| Experimental | Clear purpose, owner, limitations and at least one example or proposed test. |
| Testing | Representative tests, recorded results, verification method and known failures. |
| Approved | Named approval, bounded use, completed quality review and scheduled review date. |
| Deprecated | Reason, date and replacement or migration guidance where available. |

The repository does not yet define institutional approval roles, review intervals or data classifications. Those decisions remain with CIE and Cerebral Circuit.
