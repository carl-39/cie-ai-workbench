# Academic writing and APA

This collection brings together coaching and learning support for social-science academic writing, APA 7 referencing, and the presentation of tables and figures.

All listed assets are experimental. Learners remain responsible for their work, and educators or authorised reviewers make decisions about assessment requirements and fitness for submission.

## Choose a route

- Use the [Academic writing coach](../../skills/academic-writing-coach/SKILL.md) for developmental feedback, guided revision or focused writing practice.
- Use [Teach APA 7 microlearning](../../skills/teach-apa7-microlearning/SKILL.md) to learn or practise one referencing convention at a time.
- Use the [Table and figure assistant](../../skills/table-and-figure-assistant/SKILL.md) to create or review APA 7 tables, figures, notes, callouts and source treatment.

## Important boundaries

These skills support learning and revision; they do not produce evidence of authorship, determine misconduct or guarantee that work meets a local rubric. Standard APA 7 guidance, optional design improvements and institutional instructions must remain visibly distinct. Genuine sources, copyright status and permission claims require appropriate verification.
