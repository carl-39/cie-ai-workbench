# Collections

Collections provide project- and function-based routes through the CIE AI Workbench. They bring related prompts, skills, agents and patterns together without duplicating or relocating the canonical assets.

Use a collection when you know the work you are trying to do. Use the asset-type indexes when you are looking for a particular kind of reusable component or contributing a new asset.

## How collections work

- A collection is a curated navigation page, not a new asset type.
- The same asset may appear in several collections when it serves several kinds of work.
- Canonical content remains in `prompts/`, `skills/`, `agents/` and `patterns/`.
- Collection pages link to canonical files and do not copy their instructions or metadata.
- Adding an asset to a collection does not change its status or imply CIE approval.

## Add or update a collection

Create a descriptive kebab-case directory containing a `README.md`. State the collection's purpose and intended users, then group links by the role each asset plays in the work. Keep the page concise and update it when linked assets are renamed, deprecated or replaced.
