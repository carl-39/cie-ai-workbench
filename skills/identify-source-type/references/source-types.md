# Source-type and template guide

Use this guide as a routing aid, not as a substitute for checking current authoritative APA guidance when a source is unusual or high stakes.

## Contents

- Textual works
- Grey literature and academic works
- Data, software, and technical materials
- Audiovisual and online works
- Ambiguity checks

## Textual works

| Source type | Decisive signals | Field-based APA 7 template |
|---|---|---|
| Journal article | Journal title, volume, issue, pages/article number; publisher journal record | Author. (Year). Article title. *Journal Title, volume*(issue), pages/article number. DOI or URL |
| Advance online article | Journal record but no volume, issue, or pages yet | Author. (Year). Article title. *Journal Title*. Advance online publication. DOI |
| Preprint | Explicit preprint/version label; preprint server; not the journal version of record | Author. (Year). *Title* [Preprint]. Repository. DOI or URL |
| Magazine article | Magazine title and issue/date; general-interest periodical | Author. (Year, Month Day). Article title. *Magazine Title, volume*(issue), pages. URL |
| Newspaper article | Newspaper title and full date | Author. (Year, Month Day). Article title. *Newspaper Title*. URL |
| Authored book | Whole monograph with author(s), edition, publisher, ISBN often present | Author. (Year). *Book title* (Edition). Publisher. DOI or URL |
| Edited book | Whole book credited primarily to editor(s) | Editor (Ed.). (Year). *Book title*. Publisher. DOI or URL |
| Chapter in edited book | Chapter author/title plus editor, book title, and chapter page range | Chapter author. (Year). Chapter title. In Editor (Ed.), *Book title* (pp. xx–xx). Publisher. DOI or URL |
| Reference-work entry | Entry within dictionary, encyclopedia, or similar reference work | Author. (Year). Entry title. In Editor (Ed.), *Reference work* (Edition). Publisher. URL |
| Webpage | Discrete page on a site without a more specific publication form | Author/group. (Year, Month Day). *Page title*. Site Name. URL |
| Web article/news post | Dated article native to a website rather than a periodical issue | Author. (Year, Month Day). *Article title*. Site Name. URL |

## Grey literature and academic works

| Source type | Decisive signals | Field-based APA 7 template |
|---|---|---|
| Report | Issuing organization, report identity/number, title page, series, or formal publication context | Author/group. (Year). *Report title* (Report No. xxx). Publisher. URL |
| Government report | Government department or agency as author/issuer | Agency. (Year). *Report title* (Report No. xxx). Publisher if different. URL |
| Thesis or dissertation, published | Degree type, institution, and database/archive publication | Author. (Year). *Title* [Doctoral dissertation or master’s thesis, Institution]. Database/Archive. URL |
| Thesis or dissertation, unpublished | Degree type and institution; not in a database/archive | Author. (Year). *Title* [Unpublished doctoral dissertation or master’s thesis]. Institution. |
| Conference paper, published proceedings | Paper published within proceedings with editor/publisher/pages or DOI | Author. (Year). Paper title. In Editor (Ed.), *Proceedings title* (pp. xx–xx). Publisher. DOI or URL |
| Conference presentation | Event name, location, dates, and presentation form; no proceedings publication | Author. (Year, Month Day–Day). *Title* [Conference presentation]. Conference, Location. URL |
| Working paper | Series/institution and working-paper number or explicit designation | Author. (Year). *Title* (Working Paper No. xxx). Institution. DOI or URL |

## Data, software, and technical materials

| Source type | Decisive signals | Field-based APA 7 template |
|---|---|---|
| Dataset | Repository record, dataset label, version, DOI | Author. (Year). *Title* (Version) [Data set]. Publisher/Repository. DOI or URL |
| Software/app | Named software product, version, developer/publisher | Author. (Year). *Title* (Version) [Computer software/Mobile app]. Publisher. URL |
| Standard | Standards body and standard number | Organization. (Year). *Title* (Standard No. xxx). URL |
| Test, scale, or inventory | Standalone measurement instrument and publisher/retrieval source | Author. (Year). *Title* [Measurement instrument]. Publisher/Repository. URL |

## Audiovisual and online works

| Source type | Decisive signals | Field-based APA 7 template |
|---|---|---|
| Online video | Uploader, exact date, video title, platform | Author/uploader. (Year, Month Day). *Title* [Video]. Platform. URL |
| Film | Director, year, production company | Director (Director). (Year). *Title* [Film]. Production Company. |
| Podcast episode | Host, full date, episode title/number, series title, production company | Host (Host). (Year, Month Day). Episode title (No. x) [Audio podcast episode]. In *Podcast title*. Production Company. URL |
| Podcast series | Executive producer/host, year range, series title | Producer (Executive Producer). (Years). *Podcast title* [Audio podcast]. Production Company. URL |
| Social media post | Account identity, exact date, post text, format, platform | Author [@handle]. (Year, Month Day). *First 20 words* [Format]. Platform. URL |

## Ambiguity checks

- **Article vs webpage:** Look for a periodical identity, volume/issue, editorial publication context, and stable article record.
- **Report vs webpage:** Look for a self-contained document, issuing body, title page, report number, series, executive summary, and formal publication date.
- **Book vs report:** Look beyond ISBNs. Consider publisher role, series, authorship, intended function, and report identifiers.
- **Journal article vs preprint/repository manuscript:** Compare title, authors, DOI, journal details, version label, and dates with the publisher record.
- **Chapter vs whole book:** Determine whether the cited contribution has its own author and title within an edited container.
- **Video vs recorded webinar/conference presentation:** Classify the intellectual event when the recording documents a named presentation; otherwise use online video.
- **Dynamic webpage vs dated document:** Use a retrieval date only where content is designed to change over time and an archived version is unavailable.

APA templates vary with available elements and special cases. Preserve verified metadata, omit genuinely absent optional elements, and never manufacture placeholders in a completed reference.
